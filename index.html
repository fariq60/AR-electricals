Company Overview

AR Electricals provides engineering services for electrical control, automation and process applications. With experience dating back to 2001, our work includes bespoke control panels, manufacturing, electrical installation, panel wiring, control-system commissioning and project support.

We focus on reliable, comprehensive solutions for industrial customers, with attention to customer satisfaction, timely execution and project-specific requirements from design through installation.

Our quality approach is aligned with industry benchmarks. Performance, production and maintenance checks are followed to support consistent workmanship and dependable panel performance.
